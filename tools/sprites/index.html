Animation sprites — 256 drawn pieces

Every piece drawn for the jutsu animations. To replace one, save your image in public/img/ named exactly as shown, e.g. beastBomb-spark-red.png for the red sparks around a Tailed Beast Bomb (.png, .webp, .svg, .gif or .jpg). It takes over everywhere that piece appears. A piece marked shared is used by several jutsu; to change it for one jutsu only, name the file <jutsu id>__<piece>.png, e.g. rasengan__boom.png. Draw on a transparent background at about 2× the listed size: your image is stretched to fit that box. Pieces you haven't replaced keep the drawn version.
